How it works

A little effort. A visible difference.

1

A Pebble for your practice

Track reading, exercise, hydration, meditation, coding or tidying with an activity-specific Pebble. Log pages, repetitions, milliliters or minutes, or mark an activity done. Your Pebble responds to your entries and celebrates when you reach your goal.

2

Daily, weekly or monthly goals

Start with a daily target. Pebble Pro adds weekly and monthly goals so you can choose a period that suits your activity. Set a reminder for a time that fits your day.

3

See what you have done

Look back through your records and weekly summary. Share a progress postcard when you want to mark the moment.
