# Pebble Support

Need a hand? Email [prodottopeak@gmail.com](mailto:prodottopeak@gmail.com).

Include your device model, iOS version, Pebble version, and a short description of what happened. A screenshot can help; please leave out personal notes and other sensitive information.

## Activities and progress

### What can I track?

Pebble supports adding amounts, recording best attempts, and marking sessions complete. The available units and options are shown when you create or edit an activity; Pro options are marked in the app.

A goal period describes when progress counts toward a target. A reminder chooses when to notify you. Changing a reminder does not change the goal period.

### How do I correct a record?

Use **Undo** after recording, or open **History and details** for the activity to inspect and correct individual entries.

For an activity that adds amounts, 5 pages and 3 pages total 8 pages. For a best-attempt activity, a lower later attempt stays in history without replacing the higher result.

## Reminders and recaps

### A reminder did not arrive

1. In Pebble, check that reminders are enabled and the activity has the intended reminder time and schedule.
2. Open iPhone **Settings → Notifications → Pebble** and allow notifications.
3. Check whether Focus or Scheduled Summary is silencing or delaying alerts.
4. Reopen Pebble after changing notification permissions or schedules.

Some reminder options require Pro. Smart reminders can also be skipped when the relevant goal is already reached or a recent record makes the reminder unnecessary. Delivery and background execution are controlled by iOS; an enabled switch cannot guarantee an alert at every scheduled moment.

Reminder text can appear on the Lock Screen. You can change notification previews in iPhone Settings.

### Weekly and monthly progress recaps

Open **Pebble → Settings → Progress recaps** to manage weekly and monthly recaps. Recaps summarize recorded progress; they do not create records or mark an activity done.

Recaps also require notification permission. Open Pebble regularly so it can refresh local schedules.

## Widgets

Add a Pebble widget using the Home Screen widget gallery. If a widget looks out of date, open Pebble and check the activity first. iOS controls widget refresh timing, so updates may not appear immediately.

## Pebble Pro

### What is free?

One active activity is free. Additional active activities and the Pro options identified in the app require Pebble Pro.

### Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pebble Pro is an optional **one-time purchase**, with no recurring subscription. The App Store shows the localized price before confirmation.

### I purchased Pro, but it is not showing

Open **Pebble → Settings → Restore purchases**. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase, and make sure you have an internet connection.

Restore checks your existing purchase with Apple; it does not charge you again. If the purchase is still pending or cannot be verified, wait for Apple’s confirmation or contact support.

**Restore purchases restores Pro access, not activity records.**

## Data and recovery

### Where are my records?

Activities, entries, notes, goals, and preferences are stored locally. Pebble does not provide an in-app account, cloud sync, or a server copy from which we can recover lost records.

If you have trouble opening your data, **keep Pebble installed** and contact support before deleting or reinstalling it.

### Can I take a copy?

Open **Settings → Data and recovery → Export records** to save a readable JSON file containing activities and entries, including archived activities and notes.

Keep exported files private if they include personal information. Export is a readable copy: **importing that file back into Pebble is not currently supported**.

### Archive or delete?

Archiving keeps the activity and its history. Find it under **Settings → Archived activities**.

Permanent deletion cannot be undone. Removing the app can also remove local records. Any device-backup recovery depends on your Apple backup configuration and is not guaranteed by Pebble.
