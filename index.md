# A little progress, made visible.

<p class="lead">A few pages. A little practice. A quiet moment. Keep a record of the things that matter to you, with Pebble alongside.</p>

Pebble is an illustrated activity and progress tracker for iPhone. Choose an activity, record what you did, and see your progress take shape.

<p class="note">Preparing for launch on the App Store.</p>

## Record it your way

Add up pages, minutes, or repetitions. Keep your best attempt. Or simply mark a session done.

Reading, training, meditation, tidying, and custom activities each have a place. Start with a suggested activity and adjust its name, goal, and reminder when you need to.

## See what adds up

Keep your current result close at hand, look back through your history, and correct an entry when plans change. Optional reminders and progress recaps help you return.

Your activity records stay on your device. No app account is required.

## Start with one. Make room for more.

One active activity is free. Pebble Pro unlocks additional activities, weekly and monthly goals, and the other Pro options shown in the app.

**One-time purchase. No subscription.** The current price in your currency is shown before you buy.
