# Pebble Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Pebble is an activity and progress tracker provided by Prodotto Peak. For privacy questions, contact [prodottopeak@gmail.com](mailto:prodottopeak@gmail.com).

## At a glance

- No app account is required.
- Activity records are stored locally on your device, not on our servers.
- The app uses no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not track you across apps.
- Apple processes optional Pro purchases.
- We receive information you choose to send when you contact support.

## Activity records and preferences

Pebble stores activity names, quantities, units, goals, dates, optional notes, reminder settings, and other app preferences on your device. It uses these records to show progress, history, and recaps.

The app and its widget share relevant local data through Apple’s on-device App Group container. Activity records are not sent to us for analytics, advertising, or profiling.

Pebble does not provide its own cloud sync, online account, or server backup. Device backups, if enabled, are managed by Apple and your device settings.

## Reminders and notifications

With your permission, Pebble schedules local activity reminders, progress recaps, and app-update notices using Apple’s notification frameworks. It does not send your activity history to a push-notification service operated by us.

Notification content may include activity names or progress and may be visible on your Lock Screen. You can change permission, previews, sounds, and delivery settings in iOS Settings.

## Apple services and network requests

Pebble uses Apple’s StoreKit services to load purchase information, verify Pro access, and restore purchases. Apple handles payments; we do not receive your payment card or banking details. The app uses verified transaction information to determine whether Pro is available.

Pebble also checks Apple’s public App Store listing for compatible updates. That request includes the app’s App Store identifier and a country or region code; it does not include your activities or notes. Apple receives normal network information such as your IP address.

Review requests, App Store links, and developer-app previews use Apple services. Apple’s handling of information is described in its [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Exports and sharing

Records leave the app when you choose to export a file or share a postcard. Record exports can include notes and archived activities. Postcards share the facts shown in their preview without notes.

The destination you choose controls the shared copy. Check its contents before sharing, and keep exported files safe.

## Contacting support

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send. We use this information to respond, investigate the issue, and keep relevant support correspondence. Email is handled by our email provider.

Please do not send sensitive activity notes or full record exports unless they are necessary for an issue you want us to investigate. You can ask us to delete support correspondence by emailing the address above; we may retain information where needed to meet legal obligations or resolve an ongoing dispute.

## Retention and your choices

Local records remain until you delete them or remove the app. Archiving keeps records; permanent deletion does not. Files you export or share remain wherever you save or send them, even if you later delete the app.

You can edit or delete entries, manage archived activities, export a copy, disable notifications, and contact us about information you have sent to support. We cannot retrieve deleted local records from a Pebble server.

We do not sell personal information or share activity records for advertising.

## This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms, or tracking cookies to this website. GitHub processes request and technical information to operate its hosting service under its [Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Links to Apple or other external services are governed by those services’ policies.

## Changes

If Pebble’s handling of information changes, this policy will be updated and the date above revised.
